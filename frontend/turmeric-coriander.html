Flavor Preservation

Harvest to Home Timeline

How traditional cold-grinding & stone-pounding retain essential oils lost in high-heat commercial mills.

01. FARM HARVEST

Ethical Lifting

Harvested at peak maturity by heritage farming partners when leaves yellow naturally.

02. SUN-CURING

Natural Drying

Slow sun-drying on woven bamboo mats for 12–14 days to naturally reduce moisture below 9%.

03. COLD-GROUND

Stone Pounding

Milled under 35°C to protect volatile linalool and curcumin essential oils from heat degradation.

04. LAB QUALITY

Zero Additives

Gas chromatography tested to ensure 100% purity and zero artificial chromate or dye fillers.

05. PANTRY SEAL

Aroma-Lock Pack

Sealed in light-proof oxygen-barrier foil to deliver farm-fresh potency direct to your kitchen.

Ancient Wisdom

Ayurvedic Health & Wellness

Explore the therapeutic dosha balancing properties and active bio-compounds of single-origin spices.

Turmeric — Curcuma Longa (কাঁচা হলুদ)

Pacifies Kapha & Vata Rasa: Tikta (Bitter) & Katu (Pungent)
  • Anti-Inflammatory Powerhouse: High active curcumin content supports joint ease and cellular immunity.
  • Blood Purification (Varnya): Enhances skin radiance and natural detoxification pathways.
  • Digestive Warmth: Stimulates agni (digestive fire) without overheating the digestive tract.

Coriander — Coriandrum Sativum (ধনে)

Pacifies Pitta & Tridoshic Rasa: Madhura (Sweet) & Kashaya (Astringent)
  • Cooling & Soothing: Calms excess internal heat and digestive acidity naturally.
  • Renal & Fluid Balance: Traditional coriander tea supports kidney health and mild detoxification.
  • Linalool Aromatic Synergy: Enhances nutrient absorption when paired with turmeric and cumin.

Black Pepper — Piper Nigrum (গোলমরিচ)

Pacifies Kapha & Vata Rasa: Katu (Pungent)
  • Bioavailability Catalyst: Piperine increases curcumin absorption in turmeric by up to 2000%.
  • Respiratory Ease: Clears mucosal congestion during season transitions.
  • Metabolic Fire: Enhances nutrient assimilation across body tissues (Dhatus).
